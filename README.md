The Global Superstore Dashboard provides insights into sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, and business trends across different regions and product categories. It converts raw data into meaningful information that supports better business decisions.

The analysis is based on 51,290 orders and includes key metrics such as sales, profit, order count, discounts, yearly trends, and category performance. The company generated approximately 12.64 million in total sales and 1.47 million in profit, with an average discount rate of 14.29%.

Sales showed continuous growth from 2011 to 2014, increasing from 2.26 million to 4.30 million, indicating strong business expansion. Among product categories, Technology generated the highest sales (4.74 million), followed by Furniture and Office Supplies.

At the regional level, the APAC market recorded the highest sales performance, followed by Europe and the United States. Overall, the dashboard helps organizations monitor performance, identify trends, and make data-driven decisions for improving efficiency and profitability.
